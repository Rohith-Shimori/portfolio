Hi, I'm Rohith. I'm a Computer Science student who spends most of my time building software projects, tinkering with AI, and learning how systems work under the hood.

Rather than just reading textbooks, I learn best by writing real code. When I hit a tricky problem or want to explore new frameworks, I pair with AI tools to break down concepts, prototype ideas, and refine my understanding.

Over the past year, I've built tools like the NCC Digital Training Platform to help digitize cadet operations, created TruthLens for an AI agents capstone, and developed MVGR NexUs to make campus info easier to access.

For me, software engineering is about taking an idea, staying persistent through the bugs, and shipping something useful.
